A little clarity

Good questions.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, open checkout and enter your details. The checkout prepares a WhatsApp request. Send it in WhatsApp, then wait for stock, total and delivery confirmation. Ordering will activate when the store's number is added.

Can I pay by UPI or cash on delivery?

You can select either as your preference. The store will confirm the payment method and delivery availability before accepting the order. Only pay to the store's confirmed UPI details.

Will an accessory work with my phone?

Share your exact phone model with the store before ordering. Connector, power and fit requirements vary by product.

Are these the final products and photos?

No. This preview uses a sample catalogue and labelled illustrations. Actual photos, specifications, prices and availability will be added before launch.
